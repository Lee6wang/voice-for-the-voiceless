Use case: ads-marketing

the chosen reply returns to the real conversation as a warm voice.
Input image: The supplied collage is reference only for the approximate physical
appearance of the black rectangular Even G2 smart glasses and dark metallic Even

hands, laptop, or photography.
Scene/backdrop: Deep graphite-black minimal atmospheric space suggesting a real
intimate conversation, not a medical setting. On the far left, a softly blurred

chest, wearing one dark metallic smart ring. A small discreet smartphone may sit
lower near the torso as the source of the returning audio, without becoming a
focal point.
Visual narrative: A cool silver-white sound waveform travels from the left
speaker toward the smart glasses. At the lens area it refracts into exactly four
distinct translucent HUD response panes or glass-like shards, arranged clearly

the ring. That selected possibility changes into a warm coral-orange/gold
waveform and flows back into the shared conversational space. Use an elegant
reversed S-curve flow. Add restrained kaleidoscopic glass refraction only around
the four choices.
Style/medium: Sophisticated cinematic editorial key art, realistic people and

polished, natural anatomy, believable eyewear and ring.
Composition/framing: Wide 16:9. Reserve the left third as calm negative space for
typography while keeping the blurred partner at the extreme edge. Place the
wearer and the four HUD choices center-right. Keep every important subject and
title inside the central 70% safe area. Strong single focal point, readable at
thumbnail size.
Lighting/mood: Soft directional rim light, intimate, hopeful, dignified, quietly
defiant. The wearer appears present and self-possessed, never sad or helpless.
Color palette: Deep graphite and near-black background, silver-white incoming
sound, muted cyan/teal HUD, coral-orange to warm gold selected reply, natural
skin tones.
Text (verbatim, typeset exactly and legibly on the left):
“无声之声”
“Voice for the Voiceless”
“让每一个想表达的人，都能自己选择如何被听见。”
Small theme label: “REVERSE · ADVENTUREX 2026”
Typography: modern restrained sans-serif, Chinese title largest, English title
smaller, slogan compact; crisp off-white text with a subtle coral accent, no
decorative distortion.
Constraints: exactly four HUD choices; only one smart ring; one pair of smart
glasses; accurate natural hands and fingers; the selected response must visually
originate from human confirmation, not autonomous AI. No logos or trademarks on
devices.
Avoid: disability symbols, medical imagery, pity, sadness, crying, generic AI
brains, robots, UNO cards, Truman Show imagery, literal reverse arrows, excessive

extra fingers, watermarks, borders.
